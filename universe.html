The Network & NeuroCity

NeuroCity is the largest virtual metropolis within the Infinity Cyberpunk Universe. In 2099 the majority of human experience is routed through layered VR sectors and neural meshes. The city hosts over 120 million connected minds across multiple sectors; an unknown additional population lives outside the domes and in the undercity where connection is intermittent.

Factions & Groups

Key social and political groups in the Network:

  • The Fallen — anarchist faction that created the neural virus; anti-technology zealots operating from hidden VR enclaves.
  • The Shadows — covert protectors and information operatives; guardians of the Black Library, a secret archive of classified Network history.
  • The Sliders — the namesake creative collective and in-universe faction: individuals who "slide" between data layers, able to perceive glitches and weak points and to nudge the code with practiced intent.
  • The Dragons — power faction dominating VR-Hong Kong; code-mercenaries and information brokers who treat code as weaponized craft.
  • The Heralds — fast-moving couriers and signal-runners who transmit alerts, rumors and viral triggers across sectors.
  • The Outcasts — the undercity communities split into two cultural groups: Body-Outcasts (survivors living physically under the city, low-tech) and On-Code Outcasts (digital-only personas that exist purely within the Network).

Corporations & Industry

The economy of the Network is driven by competing companies. At the center is NeuroSystems, but the corporate landscape is plural and contested. Major corporate players include:

  • NeuroSystems — the primary architect of the Network infrastructure.
  • MutaGen — biotech and gene-simulation conglomerate (bio-augmentation & virtual physiology).
  • Security Corp — private security and counter-intrusion services for corporate clients and city sectors.
  • NeuroMedical — neural healthcare, implant maintenance, and emergency AI-assisted treatments.
  • DigitalArt — immersive entertainment and AR/VR content studio controlling major cultural channels.
  • CyberFood — synthetic nutrition and delivery networks optimized for neural schedules.
  • EnergyTech — power generation and distribution, including microgrid control across sectors.
  • Media Control — corporate aggregator for news streams, reputation metrics and ad-driven narratives.

These companies operate as rivals rather than simple subsidiaries — each controls its own networks, assets and private security.

Key Figures

The universe features many named characters and faction leaders (detailed profiles preserved in the GDD). Primary figures available now include:

  • Raven Storm — a consciousness that migrated fully into the Network and now acts as a guardian force.
  • Elias Virt — an influential architect-figure who assumed the surname Virt; linked to secret network designs.

(Full list of leaders and expanded bios available in the project GDD and reserved wiki pages.)

The Black Library

Operated by The Shadows, the Black Library is a network vault containing historical records, forbidden experiments, and partial blueprints of early Network architecture. Access is restricted and often appears as a quest location in narrative episodes.

Messenger Prototype & Tools

A secure anonymous messenger is currently in alpha. Built around the same world logic, it combines encrypted communication, immersive VR browsing and slot-based identity simulation. The client is under UI redesign before public release.
