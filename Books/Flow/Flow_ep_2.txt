Flow

Their feelings rise slowly, inexorably — like water filling a vessel. Every drop brings closer the moment when everything overflows: the silence, the glances, the unspoken words. Nika and Vika both know: one movement, one sound, and the fragile balance will shatter. But what will happen when the flow finally breaks free? And can anything stop it?

Chapter 1: Forbidden Fruit 

The office smelled of coffee and her perfume — the same one that now filled my dreams at night. I sat at my desk trying to read a report. Letters formed words, words formed sentences, and I didn't understand a single one.

She walked in wearing a plum-colored dress. Tight. New. I noticed immediately — because I was looking at her before I could stop myself.

— Vika, do you mind if I put on some music?

I nodded.

Jazz. Slow. A saxophone held a note, and it hung in the air like a thread.
